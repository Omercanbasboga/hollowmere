Hollowmere

Hollowmere used to be a keep. Now it's mostly rubble and things that shouldn't move. You're the last Warden left, and the wards that kept the depths shut are failing floor by floor. Match three of the same creature to weaken it, fill its ward meter, and seal it before your moves run out.

Board and move budget come from a small live-ops config service. If it's unreachable this still plays fine on defaults.
